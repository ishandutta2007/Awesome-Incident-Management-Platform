# Awesome-Incident-Management-Platform

## Top Incident Management Platforms (EHS & Operational) Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on EHS Incidents, Safety Events, IT/SRE Incidents, On-Call, Response Workflows & Post-Incident Learning*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Incident Management** across two related domains: **EHS (Environment, Health & Safety)** incident and event management, and **IT/SRE operational incident** response. These systems capture incidents, manage response and investigation, support regulatory reporting (EHS), and enable on-call, escalation, and postmortems (IT).



**Examples** include Intelex, Cority, VelocityEHS, ProcessMAP, EHS Insight, Sphera, Ideagen EHS, EcoOnline, Evotix, Origami Risk, Benchmark Gensuite, Enablon, Quentic, Safetymint (EHS), and PagerDuty, Opsgenie, FireHydrant, Rootly, Incident.io, Blameless, BigPanda, xMatters, Squadcast, Jira Service Management (IT/SRE).



**Open-source emphasis**: EHS incident platforms are mostly commercial. IT incident management has stronger open options (**Grafana OnCall** heritage, **Regen**, and related on-call/incident tools). General case and workflow systems can support both. This section lists the strongest available open resources.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

  Configurable EHSQ platform widely used for incident management, audits, risk, and quality workflows.



- **[Cority](https://www.cority.com/)**

- **[VelocityEHS](https://www.velocityehs.com/)**  

  Connected EHS platform covering safety, ergonomics, chemical management, and incident/event management with AI-enabled features.



- **[ProcessMAP](https://www.processmap.com/)**

- **[EHS Insight](https://www.ehsinsight.com/)**

- **[EcoOnline](https://www.ecoonline.com/)**

- **[Evotix (formerly RiskAware / similar)](https://www.evotix.com/)**  

  EHS software supporting incident, risk, and compliance management for organizations seeking digital safety programs.



- **[Origami Risk](https://www.origamirisk.com/)**  

  Risk and claims/incident management platform used by insurers, captives, and self-insured organizations.



- **[Benchmark Gensuite](https://www.gensuite.com/)**  

  EHS and sustainability platform offering incident management, audits, and compliance workflows.



- **[Enablon (Wolters Kluwer)](https://www.enablon.com/)**

- **[Quentic](https://www.quentic.com/)**

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Regen (FluidifyAI)](https://github.com/FluidifyAI/Regen)**  

  Open-source on-call and incident management platform—alerts, incidents, AI post-mortems; self-hosted alternative to PagerDuty and incident.io.



- **[Grafana OnCall (and community successors)](https://github.com/grafana/oncall)**  

  Open-source on-call management historically part of Grafana; still referenced and forked for self-hosted alerting and escalation.



- **[OpenSRE and AI incident investigation frameworks](https://github.com/)**  

  Open projects that automate incident investigation, evidence correlation, and remediation suggestions across observability tools.



- **[Homie / community on-call open tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight open on-call and escalation schedulers used by smaller SRE teams.



- **[Open case and incident tracking systems](https://github.com/)**  

  General open case-management and ticketing tools adapted for EHS incident or IT incident workflows.



- **[ERPNext / Odoo EHS and maintenance modules](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Open ERP modules that can support incident/near-miss recording, corrective actions, and basic safety workflows.



- **[Forms and observation open apps for EHS](https://github.com/)**  

  Mobile-friendly open form builders used for safety observations, near-miss, and incident intake.



- **[Status page open projects (Cachet, etc.)](https://github.com/)**  

  Open status-page tools often paired with incident response for external communication.



- **[Runbook and playbook open repositories](https://github.com/)**  

  Structured open collections of response procedures linked from incident tools.



- **[Postmortem template and learning open kits](https://github.com/)**  

  Templates and small apps for blameless post-incident reviews and action tracking.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Deploying **Regen** or Grafana OnCall-style stacks for self-hosted IT incident and on-call management.

- Using **open forms + case tools** for basic EHS incident and observation reporting in smaller organizations.

- Combining open on-call with commercial EHS platforms when safety and IT incidents must be linked.

- Accepting that full EHS regulatory modules, process safety, occupational health depth, and enterprise audit trails still favor commercial EHS platforms (Intelex, Cority, VelocityEHS, Sphera, Enablon, etc.).

- Accepting that global on-call scale, advanced AIOps, and vendor SLAs still favor commercial IT incident platforms (PagerDuty, incident.io, Rootly, FireHydrant, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, cost control, and transparent response workflows.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

- **EHS**: Capture incidents/observations via open forms → track in a case system → manage CAPA → report for compliance.  

- **IT/SRE**: Ingest alerts → route via open on-call → coordinate in chat → run open postmortems → feed improvements back to runbooks.  

Suitable for teams with ops capacity. Most enterprises adopt commercial platforms for EHS compliance and for production-grade on-call reliability.
